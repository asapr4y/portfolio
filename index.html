Capstone project / Esports HRIS

Tyrant AIM

Tyrant AIM (AI Management) is our team’s capstone project—an AI-powered HRIS developed for Tyrant Esports. It centralizes player records, attendance, contracts, and payroll, alongside match-stat extraction from MLBB and COD Mobile screenshots, performance analytics, optimized practice scheduling, and rule-based coaching recommendations. My contributions focused on adding and refining frontend elements, performing manual QA testing, and troubleshooting issues using browser DevTools and AI-assisted development tools.
